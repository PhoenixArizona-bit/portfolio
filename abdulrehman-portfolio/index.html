About me

One person,
the whole stack

I build solo, on purpose. No team, no funding round — just a laptop, a few hours between classes, and a habit of shipping instead of endlessly planning.

Every project starts the same way: spot a real gap in a local market, build the smallest version that actually works, and get it in front of real people fast. Zentro exists because booking a barber in Lahore was still happening over phone calls.

I work mostly in React and Supabase, and along the way I've picked up motion design, video editing, and scrappy growth marketing — when you're solo, you own every part of the product.
